# Family Update Sheet

Print this sheet to draft a short update for a client's family. Check with your
supervisor first: are family updates allowed, how should you send them, and what
may you share?

**Never send a family update through AI with real client details.**

**AI prompt to turn this into a message**

Copy and paste this into your free AI tool. Use placeholders only:

> "I am a home care aide writing a short update to a client's family. Turn my
> rough points into a warm, short message of 3 to 5 sentences. Plain English,
> friendly and respectful. No medical advice, no diagnoses, no medication
> details. End with how to reach the agency.
>
> Rough points:
> [Paste your filled-in sheet here, placeholders only]"

**Safety:** Send only through your agency's approved channel. When in doubt,
keep it short and general.
