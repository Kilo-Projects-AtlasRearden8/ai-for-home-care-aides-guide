# Quick-Start Cheat Sheet

Keep this one-pager near your workstation or on the fridge.

---

## The three golden rules

1. **Education only.** AI is not medical advice. Never change a care plan or
   give health advice based on AI.
2. **Never paste client details.** No names, birthdates, addresses, diagnoses,
   medications, or IDs. Use [Client] placeholders when practicing.
3. **You are responsible.** Read everything AI writes before you use it. AI can
   make things up.

## Before you touch AI

☐ Read `docs/00-safety-first.md`
☐ Ask your supervisor if AI documentation is allowed at your agency
☐ Pick a free tool (`docs/05-free-models.md`)
☐ Print a sheet from `docs/printable-sheets/`

## Writing with AI — 4 steps

1. Jot down facts on paper first (never real client details).
2. Paste a prompt into your free AI tool (see examples in each chapter).
3. Read the result out loud. Fix anything wrong or invented.
4. Save the finished note to your agency's system — not in the AI chat.

## Daily note sections

- Visit details (who, date, times)
- Tasks completed
- How the client seemed
- Things to report
- Plan for next visit

## Family update sections

- Short greeting
- How the day went
- Something positive noticed
- Changes to watch for
- How to reach the agency
- Kind closing

## Care plan reminders

- Care plans come from nurses or your agency — not AI.
- AI can summarize or make a checklist; never change medical details.
- Show any AI summary to your supervisor before using it.
