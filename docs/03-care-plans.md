# Care Plans with AI

A care plan is the roadmap for how a client is cared for. It usually comes from a
nurse, care manager, or your agency. AI cannot create or change a care plan —
only licensed professionals can do that.

**AI can help you summarize, organize, and rewrite parts of a care plan so they
are easier to read. AI cannot make clinical decisions.**

Never paste a real care plan — or any client details — into an AI tool.

## What you can (and cannot) use AI for with care plans

| You CAN do this | You CANNOT do this |
|---|---|
| Summarize a care plan in plain English for your own reference | Change medications, doses, or schedules |
| Rewrite family-facing parts in warmer, simpler language | Diagnose a condition or suggest treatments |
| Make a checklist from a care plan so nothing gets missed | Tell a family member what to do medically |
| Practice with fake care plans to learn the format | Share real client information with AI |

## Step-by-step: summarize a care plan for yourself

### Step 1: Start with the official care plan

Get the care plan from your agency or supervisor. Read it yourself first. AI is
a helper, not a replacement for reading the plan.

### Step 2: De-identify before anything goes near AI

Copy the parts you want to summarize into a document. Remove:

- Client name and family names
- Address and phone number
- Birthdate and ID numbers
- Diagnosis names (use general terms like "mobility support" instead)
- Medication names and doses

Replace them with placeholders like [Client], [Diagnosis], [Medication].

### Step 3: Paste a summary prompt

> "Summarize this care plan in plain English for a home care aide. Use short
> sentences and bullet points. Include: daily goals, tasks the aide helps with,
> things to watch for, and when to call a supervisor. Do not add medical advice.
>
> [Paste de-identified care plan here, with placeholders only]"

### Step 4: Check the summary

- Does it match the real care plan?
- Did AI leave anything out?
- Did AI add anything not in the plan? Remove invented details.
- Show the summary to your supervisor if you plan to use it at work.

### Step 5: File it properly

Save the summary where your agency keeps care-plan references. Do not leave it
in the AI chat.

## Step-by-step: make a daily checklist from a care plan

> "Turn this care plan into a simple daily checklist for a home care aide.
> One line per task, with checkboxes. Plain English. No medical advice.
>
> [De-identified care plan with placeholders only]"

Print the checklist and carry it with you. Check off tasks as you complete them.

## Safety reminders

- The care plan from your agency or nurse is the real one. AI summaries are
  only a convenience — never let an AI summary override the official plan.
- If AI suggests anything medical, ignore it and ask your supervisor.
- Never paste a client's real care plan into AI.
