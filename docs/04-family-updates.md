# Family Updates

Families want to know how their loved one is doing. A short, warm update goes a
long way. AI can help you write one quickly — but you provide the real
observations, and you never share private details without permission.

## What a good family update includes

- A short greeting
- How the day went (general, not clinical)
- Something positive or specific you noticed
- Any changes to watch for
- How to reach you or the agency
- A kind closing

## Step-by-step: write a family update with AI

### Step 1: Check permission first

Ask your supervisor whether family updates are allowed, how they should be sent
(text, email, agency app), and what you may share.

### Step 2: Jot down what you want to say

Use made-up placeholders while practicing:

> Greeting for [Family Member]'s family.
> [Client] had a good day today. We went for a short walk.
> Client ate most of lunch and seemed cheerful.
> Client slept well last night.
> Nothing new to worry about.
> You can call the agency at [Phone] with questions.
> Warmly, [Aide Name]

### Step 3: Paste a prompt into your free AI tool

> "I am a home care aide writing a short update to a client's family. Turn my
> rough points into a warm, short message — about 3 to 5 sentences. Plain
> English. No medical advice. No diagnoses. No medication details.
>
> Rough points:
> [Paste your placeholders here]
>
> Keep it friendly and respectful. End with how to reach the agency."

### Step 4: Read it out loud

Does it sound like you? Is it warm but not too casual? Fix anything that sounds
off. Make sure no real names or private details slipped in.

### Step 5: Send it the approved way

Send through your agency's approved channel only. Do not text from your personal
phone unless your supervisor has said it is OK.

## Example practice update (completely fake)

```
Hi [Family Member],

[Client] had a nice day today. We took a short walk together and [Client] ate
most of lunch. [Client] seemed cheerful and slept well last night. Nothing new
to report.

If you have any questions, you can reach the agency at [Phone].

Warmly,
[Aide Name]
```

## Safety reminders

- Never send a family update with real client details through AI.
- Check with your supervisor before sharing anything about a client with family.
- When in doubt, keep the update short and general.
