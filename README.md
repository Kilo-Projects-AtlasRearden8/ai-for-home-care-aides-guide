Daily notes, care plans, and family updates using free AI models.

and updates for family members — without spending money on software.

Written in plain English, step by step, with printable sheets you can keep at
work or stick on the fridge. No coding required. No experience with AI needed.

## How to use this manual

1. **Read the safety rules first.** (`docs/00-safety-first.md`, or `site/safety.html`)
2. **Pick a free AI tool.** (`docs/05-free-models.md`, or `site/free-models.html`)
3. **Follow the step-by-step guide** for daily notes, care plans, or family updates.
4. **Print a sheet** from `docs/printable-sheets/` or `site/printable-sheets/` and fill it in by hand before you ever open an AI tool.

## The three golden rules

1. **This is education only.** Nothing here is medical advice, nursing advice, or a substitute for your care plan, your supervisor, or a licensed clinician.
2. **Never paste client details into an AI tool.** No names, no birthdates, no addresses, no diagnosis details, no IDs. Use made-up placeholders like "[Client]" when practicing.
3. **You remain responsible.** AI can make mistakes. Always read over anything AI writes before you use it.

Follow your employer's policies and HIPAA rules. When in doubt, ask your supervisor before using AI with anything related to a client.
