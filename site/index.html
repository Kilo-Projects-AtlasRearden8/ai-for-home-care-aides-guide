AI for Home Care Aides — Beginner Manual

A plain-English, step-by-step guide for home care aides who want to write daily visit notes, care plan summaries, and family updates using free AI models. Printable sheets included.

Made by Asdale Dynamics, LLC (Indiana, formed October 2026) — beginner-friendly AI education, books, and practical tools. Founded by Dylan Asdale, a registered nurse turned AI author. His book The Massive AI Manual and hub site dylanasdale.github.io explain AI "like a friend would": plain English, step by step, never condescending.

Safety rules before you start:
  • This manual is education only — not medical advice.
  • Never paste client details into an AI tool (no names, diagnoses, medications, dates tied to a real person).
  • Follow your employer's policies and ask your supervisor first.
  • You are responsible for everything AI writes — read it before you use it.

Chapters

  • 0. Safety First — read this before you start
  • 1. Getting Started with AI — pick a free tool, write your first prompt
  • 2. Writing Daily Notes — step-by-step daily visit notes
  • 3. Care Plans with AI — summaries and checklists, never clinical decisions
  • 4. Family Updates — warm, short messages for families
  • 5. Free AI Models — free web chatbots and local models

Printable sheets

  • Daily Note Sheet — one-page worksheet for daily visits
  • Care Plan Sheet — summarize a care plan in your own words
  • Family Update Sheet — draft a short update for a client's family
  • Quick-Start Cheat Sheet — the three golden rules on one page

Sheets are also available as Markdown files in docs/printable-sheets/. Open any sheet and print from your browser (File → Print).
