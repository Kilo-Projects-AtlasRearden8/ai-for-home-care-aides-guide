Safety First: Read This Before You Start

This manual is education only. It is not medical advice.

AI can help you write faster, but it cannot replace your training, your care plan, your supervisor, or a licensed clinician. Never use AI to make medical decisions, change a care plan, or give health advice to a client or family member.

The golden rules

  1. Never paste client details into an AI tool. No names, no birthdates, no addresses, no phone numbers, no diagnoses, no medication names or doses, and no ID numbers. Assume anything you type into a free AI tool could be seen by others.
  2. Practice with made-up details only. Every example in this manual uses fake names like [Client] and fake situations. Replace them with nothing real.
  3. Follow your employer's policies. Some agencies do not allow AI for client documentation at all. Ask your supervisor before you use AI with anything related to a client.
  4. You are responsible for everything you submit. AI can hallucinate (make things up) and can miss important details. Read every word AI writes before you use it. If something looks wrong, fix it or throw it away.
  5. When in doubt, leave it out. If you are not sure whether something is safe to type into an AI tool, do not type it. Write it by hand instead.

What "client details" means (examples of what NOT to paste)

  • Client name, initials, or nickname
  • Family members' names
  • Address, room number, or facility name
  • Birthdate or age
  • Diagnosis or medical condition
  • Medication names, doses, or times
  • Insurance or ID numbers
  • Specific dates of visits combined with the client's identity

What IS safe to paste

  • Completely made-up examples (like the ones in this manual)
  • General questions about writing, grammar, or tone ("How do I make this sentence sound warmer?")
  • Public information that cannot be tied to any person
De-identifying a real note: remove every name, date, and place first, then read it over to make sure the client could not be identified from what remains. When in doubt, do not paste it.
