Getting Started with AI

AI tools are computer programs that can read and write in something close to plain English. You type a request (called a prompt) and the tool writes back a response. You do not need to know how to code.

This chapter walks you through picking a free tool, making an account safely, and writing your first prompt.

Step 1: Pick a free AI tool

You do not need to pay for AI. Several good tools have free versions. See Free AI Models for a short, beginner-friendly list.

Good starter options for home care aides:

  • A free web chatbot you can open in your phone or computer browser
  • A local AI model that runs on your own computer (nothing leaves your device)

Step 2: Make an account without sharing personal data

If the tool asks for an account:

  • Use an email address you already have. You do not need to give your work email.
  • Do not use your client's name, your client's address, or anything related to your clients when you sign up.
  • Do not pay for a plan unless your employer has reviewed and approved it.

Step 3: Write your first prompt

A prompt is just a request in plain English. Be specific about:

  • Who you are (a home care aide writing a visit note)
  • What you need (a daily note, a care plan summary, a family update)
  • What to include (the sections you want, the tone, how long)
  • What NOT to include (real client names, diagnoses, medication details)
Example practice prompt (fake details only): 
> I am a home care aide. Help me write a short daily visit note for a made-up client named [Client]. Include these sections: arrival time, tasks completed, how the client seemed, and anything to report. Keep it to one paragraph per section. Use plain English and a warm, respectful tone. Do not add any medical advice.

The AI will write something back. Read it, edit it, and make it your own.

Step 4: Save your work outside the AI tool

Copy the finished note into your agency's documentation system, a notebook, or a printed sheet. Do not rely on the AI tool as your only copy — you may lose access to the chat later.

Your first practice exercise

Open your chosen AI tool and try the practice prompt above. Then:

  1. Read the AI's response out loud.
  2. Fix anything that sounds wrong or too formal.
  3. Confirm there are no real names, dates, or medical details in it.
  4. Save it to a file or print it out.

If you can do those four things, you are ready for the next chapters.
